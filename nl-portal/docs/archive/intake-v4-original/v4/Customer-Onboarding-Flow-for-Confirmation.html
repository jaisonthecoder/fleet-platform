How a customer joins the portal

Nobody signs up on their own. Our team sets the company up from the records we already hold in CargoWise, then the company sets up its own people. Example used throughout: Gulf Fresh Trading, Dubai office, served by the Noatum Dubai branch.

Noatum does it The customer does it CargoWise, read only The portal does it automatically

Onboarding one customer is a few minutes of screen work. The waiting is on the human side: the customer nominating an administrator, and that person accepting.

Three things worth knowing

Nobody registers themselves

There is no sign-up page. A company joins because we onboard it; a person joins because someone invited them. Anyone can still use public tracking and the CO2 calculator without an account.

We do not re-type the customer

The company already exists in CargoWise. We import that list daily, search it, and pick the right offices. Only the portal-specific settings — tier, email domain, features — are entered by hand.

Growing is set-up, not development

Adding another office, another customer, or another branch is the same few screens. Features are switched on customer by customer.
