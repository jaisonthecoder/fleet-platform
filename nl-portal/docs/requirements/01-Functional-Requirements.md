## 1. Purpose and specification convention

This specification describes the required product behaviour across all planned delivery phases. It follows an ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018-style requirements structure: scope, stakeholders, interfaces, uniquely identified requirements, verification criteria and traceability. It is not a claim of certification or a reproduction of the standard. NFRs specify the cross-cutting quality constraints separately.

Each catalogue row is a controlled requirement with an immutable ID, a normative **shall** statement, delivery allocation, source and acceptance criterion. Test cases shall reference these IDs. Catalogue-level owner is Product; Identity, Operations, Pricing, Finance and Integration owners review their relevant sections. Verification is functional test and business demonstration unless the row specifies inspection or integration evidence. Implementation status for every row is **not assessed as delivered**.

Delivery notation: **P1** foundation and quotation; **P2** visibility; **P3** booking and claims; **P4** intelligence and analytics; **P5** ecosystem; **Deferred** outside committed delivery until reinstated. These are delivery phases, not the PRD's P0/P1/P2 priority labels. Unless labelled conditional or deferred, a row is required for its allocated phase's release. Existing PRD priorities remain available through the Source column; a fast-follow capability may be separately released but cannot silently disappear.

Source aliases: **PRD** = [PRD v4.0](../PRD-Noatum-Customer-Platform.md); **KICK** = [kickoff plan](../Project-Kickoff-and-Phase1-Plan.md); **ROOT** = [repository rules](../../CLAUDE.md); **SIMPL** = [simplification register](../Simplification-Register.md); **UC** = user clarifications recorded in the [index](README.md). Decision IDs refer to the index's decision register. New proposed details are explicitly identified; they require approval before becoming a build baseline.

## 2. Product boundary

The portal is the customer window onto CW1 and a channel for customer quote and booking requests. CW1 remains the operational system of record. The portal owns customer grouping, organisation links, application roles and permissions, preferences, audit, and the approved portal workflows. It holds synchronised canonical projections, not a replacement operational system.

**UC-12 amendment:** quotation publication is controlled by an authorised administrator: either CW1 business confirmation is required or staff may publish directly without submitting the priced offer to CW1. In the direct path the portal retains the authoritative published commercial snapshot, without claiming CW1 confirmation. This does not bypass internal approval or change booking/shipment integration. Later CW1 reconciliation and configuration precedence are decision-gated under D-18. Older PRD/repository statements requiring CW1 confirmation universally must be reconciled before implementation; this amendment records the user's approved documentation direction, not a completed architecture change.

Track and trace, documents, invoices, claims and bookings are required application modules. Phase 1 includes their navigation definitions, permission catalogue, role assignment and authorised menu visibility. Later phases deliver their working screens, workflows and integrations. A menu's presence is not evidence that the module is operational.

Excluded from the current delivery baseline: Redis, Service Bus/RabbitMQ and extra broker infrastructure; CRM integration; Power BI embedding; development, training or hosting of AI models by this team; rate management; platform-owned authentication; PO lifecycle management; CW1 operational execution other than approved internal quotation pricing. CRM and Power BI are not scheduled implicitly in a later phase. AI-dependent functions consume approved other-team APIs. CO2 remains deferred.

| Customer Analyst | Standard capabilities plus assigned reports, later self-service reports and schedules |
| Customer Claims | Standard capabilities plus claim submission and comments when released |

| Noatum Customer Success | Authorised customer setup, organisation linking, invitations, support and content administration |
| Noatum Pricing / Pricing Approver | Branch-scoped pricing / independent approval; cross-branch and cost access require separate permissions |
| Noatum Ops | Authorised read visibility across customers; tracking not branch-scoped; no general CW1 operational editing |

Roles are presets of permissions, not hard-coded checks in screens or services. A user may receive several compatible roles. Internal operational assignments, including claims handling, must not be inferred from role names.

### 10.1 CO2 calculation and emissions visibility

CO2 calculation is explicitly retained in the product requirements in the three PRD locations below, plus quotation estimation as user-approved conditional future scope (UC-11). It is not an AI-model feature and must not be lost when simplifying AI or infrastructure. The governing PRD currently defers delivery; the phase is to be confirmed after OQ-20 (methodology, provider approach and legal disclaimer). This catalogue defines the required behaviour without selecting a provider, local calculation engine or emission factors. Phase 1 quotation creation and pricing do not depend on CO2 calculation.

The [CO2 knowledge-base note](../KB-CO2-Emissions-Calculator.md) provides background on the three surfaces and a common calculation service. Its older Phase 4 allocation, OQ-11 reference, local-engine choice, formulas and synchronous external-call examples are not an approved implementation baseline: PRD v4 uses OQ-20, and repository adapter/job rules still apply.
