From invitation to claim: one picture

Read it left to right like a story. The top lane is what Noatum people do. The middle lane is the customer's journey. Under each step you see which part of the portal does the work, and the arrows show what data leaves the portal and what comes back, and from where.

A step in the customer's journey What Noatum people do at that step Portal modules doing the work A system outside the portal Data flow, immediate Data flow through the queue (may take a moment)
Steps 1–2 stay insideOnboarding and team set-up create nothing in CargoWise. The portal only reads CargoWise's organisation list to know which records belong to the customer.
Steps 3–4 go downA quote request first asks the rate engine; if there is no ready rate it goes to the branch through CargoWise. A booking always goes to CargoWise and comes back with a shipment reference.
Steps 5–6 come upDocuments, invoices, milestones and ETA changes flow up from CargoWise as operations work; carrier data is merged in with a record of where it came from. The portal decides who may see each item.
Steps 7–8 fan outAlerts become bell notifications and emails; reports become exports; a claim becomes a Dynamics 365 case whose replies come back into the portal.
