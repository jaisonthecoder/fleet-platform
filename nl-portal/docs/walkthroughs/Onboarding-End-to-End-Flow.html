From an empty portal to the first quote

Ten stages, in the order they actually happen. Example throughout: the Noatum Dubai branch, customer Gulf Fresh Trading (Dubai office), first administrator Fatima, quoting user Omar.

Noatum does it The customer does it CargoWise, read only The portal does it automatically

Before any customer exists

0ONCE

The portal itself is ready

Platform Admin
  1. Noatum staff sign in with their Microsoft work account. The first Platform Admin is created by configuration. Internal users belong to no customer.
  2. Internal roles are assigned: Customer Success, Ops, Platform Admin.
  3. The portal imports the CargoWise company list daily — every company CargoWise knows, with code, name, country and branch. Nothing is a customer yet; this is only the menu to pick from.
  4. Code mapping is loaded: CargoWise status, event, charge and document-type codes become our customer-facing names.
  5. Preset customer roles exist: Standard, Quoting and Booking, Finance, Analyst, Claims, Customer Admin.
1ONCE PER BRANCH

The branch is ready — Dubai

Noatum, mostly outside the portal

There is no region screen and no region in the database. "One region" means one Noatum branch. Preparing it is four checks, three of them outside the portal.

#WhereWhat
1aCargoWiseDubai's company records appear in our imported list; Dubai's outbound messages will reach our endpoint (or the simulator for now)
1bRate sourceDubai's rates for its lanes exist where the instant-quote call looks. Without them, every quote takes the manual route
1cPeopleDubai pricing knows portal quote requests will arrive in CargoWise; Dubai operations know their milestones become visible to the customer
1dIn the portalA news banner targeted to country AE — the only region-specific setting in the portal

What Noatum does per customer

2

Create the customer

Customer Success
  1. Noatum Admin → Customers → New customer.
  2. Search "Gulf Fresh". The portal searches the imported CargoWise list; legal name and country are pre-filled.
  3. Add what CargoWise does not know: tier, email domain gulffresh.ae, and which features are switched on — quotes now, tracking later.
  4. Save. Status is Onboarding. Nobody at Gulf Fresh knows yet.
3

Connect the customer to their CargoWise records

Customer Success · one audited action with a confirm dialog
  1. Customer → Accounts → "Add organisation" → search → tick the Dubai office; Riyadh stays out of the pilot → one-line reason → Confirm.
  2. The dialog shows company name, code, country and target customer before it saves.
  3. On save: the office belongs to Gulf Fresh — one record can belong to only one customer, ever; a background job makes their existing shipments, invoices and staff-created quotes visible to them; anything unassigned for that record is cleared.
This is the most important step in the flow. It is what decides which cargo this customer can see. Open decision: should a second colleague approve it?
4

Invite the customer's administrator

Customer Success
  1. Customer → Users → "Invite Customer Admin" → Fatima, fatima@gulffresh.ae.
  2. The portal checks the email domain, generates a single-use link that expires, and sends the branded invitation.
The link goes to one named person, never a shared mailbox: it is tied to that address and the login must match it.

What the customer does

5

The administrator accepts

Fatima, at Gulf Fresh
  1. Clicks the link and sees "Gulf Fresh Trading — invited by Noatum Customer Success".
  2. Signs in through Microsoft: her company account, Gulf Fresh's own sign-on if they have one, or an email-and-password account with two-step verification that Microsoft creates. We write none of this.
  3. The portal checks the email matches the invitation, creates her user with the Customer Admin role, and opens the dashboard.
  4. Gulf Fresh flips from Onboarding to Active — one linked office, one accepted administrator.
6

The administrator builds the team

Fatima · Noatum is no longer involved

Administration → Users → Invite: Omar (Quoting and Booking), Sara (Finance), Khalid (Standard). Same link mechanism, same domain check. Fatima also fills the address book, or imports it, and saves a quote template.

7

The first quote — the pilot moment

Omar, at Gulf Fresh
  1. Request a quote: Sea LCL, FOB, Shanghai → Jebel Ali, 20 pallets, 8 tonnes, 24 CBM.
  2. Submit. The portal uses their Dubai office automatically, asks the rate source, finds Dubai's rate, and shows options with charge lines and a validity date in seconds.
  3. Omar accepts. The price is frozen, a PDF is generated, and a "Book" button appears — booking itself is Phase 3.
  4. In CargoWise a quote record appears for that office carrying our portal reference, so Dubai pricing can see it.
Had Omar asked for a lane with no rate, the request would land with Dubai pricing in CargoWise, and their price would flow back into his screen.

After that, and how it grows

8

It runs by itself

Nobody does anything
  • Every new shipment Dubai operations create for this customer becomes visible to them, once tracking is switched on.
  • Every milestone, document and invoice for those shipments follows.
  • Each office sees only the invoices addressed to it.
  • Anything arriving with a company record we have not linked is stored but shown to nobody, and listed for Customer Success.
