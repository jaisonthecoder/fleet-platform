Customer Information Platform

One portal where our customers see and do everything.

Today a customer emails or calls to find their cargo, waits for a quote by email, and asks for every document and invoice. The portal gives them one login to track, quote, book, download and be alerted, in the Noatum brand, connected to the system our operations teams already use.

Today

  • "Where is my shipment" by email and phone
  • Quotes typed by hand, sent as attachments
  • Documents and invoices requested one by one
  • Several tools, several logins, no single view

With the portal

  • Every shipment and milestone live, self-service
  • Instant price where we hold rates; otherwise routed to the branch
  • Documents and invoices downloaded by the customer
  • One login, one brand, every country

How the platform works

Five layers. The customer works on the top one. Shipment updates come in and quote requests go out only through the bottom one.

Screens never decide

Whether a customer may see a shipment or accept a quote is decided in the business-rules layer and checked again there, whatever the screen shows.

Fast because the data is already here

Shipment lists and timelines open instantly because the portal holds its own copy. New milestones from operations refresh that copy as they happen.

One connection point, many adapters

Each outside system has its own adapter in the integration layer. A new carrier feed or a different operations system later means a new adapter, not a new portal.

Why it matters

For customers

Digital-first forwarders already offer this. Customers compare us on it. One login for tracking, quotes, bookings, documents, invoices and alerts removes the daily friction of working with us.

For operations

Fewer routine enquiries into the branches. Quote requests and bookings arrive complete and land in CargoWise without re-typing. Staff keep working where they work today.

For the group

One brand experience across 25 countries, usage data on what customers value, and a foundation for CO2 reporting, predictive ETA and an AI assistant — without a second platform later.

Minimal effort, on the right things

Screens are the quick part and follow the existing mockup. Three things must be solid from the first version because they cannot be added later: keeping each customer’s data separate, the two-way connection to CargoWise, and how a customer company and its users are set up.

Not blocked by CargoWise access

A small stand-in for CargoWise lets us build and demonstrate every feature before real test access is granted, then switch over by changing a setting.

Second customer is configuration

Every feature ships switched off and is turned on per customer. Adding the next customer or region is set-up, not a new project.
